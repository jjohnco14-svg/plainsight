Choice Light has the options GREEN (0), YELLOW (1), and RED (2).

Record Crossing holds light (Light choice) and timer (whole number).

Function crossing_tick (line 16)
  Takes: c (pointer to Crossing record), button_pressed (truth value)
  Gives back: nothing
  Steps:
    1. Checks the value of the light of what c points to:
       If it is GREEN:
         1. Adds 1 to the timer of what c points to.
         2. If (the timer of what c points to) is at least GREEN_TIME (30) or (button_pressed and (the timer of what c points to) is at least MIN_GREEN (10)):
              1. Puts YELLOW into the light of what c points to.
              2. Puts 0 into the timer of what c points to.
       If it is YELLOW:
         1. Adds 1 to the timer of what c points to.
         2. If (the timer of what c points to) is at least YELLOW_TIME (5):
              1. Puts RED into the light of what c points to.
              2. Puts 0 into the timer of what c points to.
       If it is RED:
         1. Adds 1 to the timer of what c points to.
         2. If (the timer of what c points to) is at least RED_TIME (20):
              1. Puts GREEN into the light of what c points to.
              2. Puts 0 into the timer of what c points to.
       If it is none of these, nothing here happens.
