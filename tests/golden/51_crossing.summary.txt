Modes of Light: GREEN, YELLOW, or RED.

Record Crossing holds light and timer.

Function crossing_tick
  Takes: c (a Crossing record, changed in place), button_pressed (truth value)
  What it does:
    Moves light between modes, depending on its current mode:
      In GREEN:
        - every time, first: timer goes up by 1
        - if timer is at least 30 (GREEN_TIME) or (button_pressed and timer is at least 10 (MIN_GREEN)): goes to YELLOW; timer becomes 0
        - otherwise: it stays in GREEN
      In YELLOW:
        - every time, first: timer goes up by 1
        - if timer is at least 5 (YELLOW_TIME): goes to RED; timer becomes 0
        - otherwise: it stays in YELLOW
      In RED:
        - every time, first: timer goes up by 1
        - if timer is at least 20 (RED_TIME): goes to GREEN; timer becomes 0
        - otherwise: it stays in RED
      Any other value: nothing happens.
  Changes: only the record it's given (c).

Function count_rush_hours
  Takes: cars (list of whole numbers, only read), hours (whole number)
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    Goes through the items of cars, position 0 up to hours minus 1:
      - counts the items that are over 100, in rush (starting from 0)
    Then gives back rush.
  Changes: nothing outside itself.
