Function first_negative
  Takes: values (list of whole numbers, only read), n (whole number)
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    Goes through the items of values, position 0 up to n minus 1 (it may stop early):
      - stops at the first item that is below 0, and remembers its position in found (found stays -1 if there is none)
    Then gives back found.
  Changes: nothing outside itself.
