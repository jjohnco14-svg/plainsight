Record RingBuf holds items, head, and count.

Function rb_init
  Takes: rb (a RingBuf record, changed in place)
  What it does:
    - head becomes 0
    - count becomes 0
  Changes: only the record it's given (rb).

Function rb_push
  Takes: rb (a RingBuf record, changed in place), value (whole number)
  Gives back: a truth value
  What it does:
    (exact step) If count is equal to CAP (4):
         1. Stops and gives back false (0).
    (exact step) Creates tail (whole number), starting at the remainder of (head plus count) divided by CAP (4).
    - item tail of items becomes value
    - count goes up by 1
    Then gives back true.
  Changes: only the record it's given (rb).

Function rb_pop
  Takes: rb (a RingBuf record, changed in place), out (pointer to whole number)
  Gives back: a truth value
  What it does:
    (exact step) If count is equal to 0:
         1. Stops and gives back false (0).
    - the location out points to becomes item head of items
    - head becomes the remainder of (head plus 1) divided by 4 (CAP)
    - count goes down by 1
    Then gives back true.
  Changes: only what out points to and the record it's given (rb).

Function main
  Takes: nothing
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    (exact step) Creates rb (RingBuf record), not yet given a value.
    - calls rb_init with the location of rb
    (exact step) Loop:
       Before starting:
         1. Creates v (whole number), starting at 1.
       Keeps going while v is at most 5.
       After each round:
         1. Adds 1 to v.
       Each round:
         1. If not (the result of calling rb_push with the location of rb and v):
              1. Prints "push [v] refused" and starts a new line.
    (exact step) Creates x (whole number), not yet given a value.
    - calls rb_pop with the location of rb and the location of x
    - prints "[x] "
    - calls rb_pop with the location of rb and the location of x
    - prints "[x] "
    - calls rb_push with the location of rb and 6
    (exact step) While the result of calling rb_pop with the location of rb and the location of x, repeats:
         1. Prints "[x] ".
    - starts a new line
    Then gives back 0.
  Changes: no data outside itself.
  Also: reads or writes input/output (printf); calls rb_init, rb_pop, and rb_push (their effects aren't included here yet).
