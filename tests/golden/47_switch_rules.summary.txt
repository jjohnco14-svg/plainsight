Modes of Level: LOW, MID, or HIGH.

Function classify
  Takes: code (whole number)
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    (exact step) Creates score (whole number), starting at 0.
    (exact step) Checks the value of code:
       If it is 0 or 1:
         1. Sets score to 5.
       If it is 2:
         1. Sets score to 7.
         2. Then continues straight into the next case's steps (there is no break here).
       If it is 3:
         1. Adds 1 to score.
         2. If score is greater than 7:
              1. Leaves the case check (goes on to the step after it).
         3. Sets score to 0.
       In any other case:
         1. Sets score to negative 1.
    Then gives back score.
  Changes: nothing outside itself.

Function count_high
  Takes: levels (list of whole numbers, only read), n (whole number)
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    (exact step) Creates highs (whole number), starting at 0.
    (exact step) Repeats n times, with i counting from 0:
         1. Checks the value of item i of levels:
            If it is HIGH:
              1. Adds 1 to highs.
            If it is LOW:
              1. Skips the rest of this round and moves to the next round.
            If it is none of these, nothing here happens.
    Then gives back highs.
  Changes: nothing outside itself.
