Function find
  Takes: a (list of whole numbers, only read), n (whole number), x (whole number)
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    Goes through the items of a, position 0 up to n minus 1 (it may stop early):
      - stops at the first item that is x, and gives back its position
    If it gets through every item without stopping, gives back negative 1.
  Changes: nothing outside itself.

Function check_all
  Takes: a (list of whole numbers, only read), n (whole number)
  What it does:
    (exact step) Repeats up to n times, with i counting from 0:
         1. If (item i of a) is less than 0:
              1. Ends the whole program with status 1.
  Changes: no data outside itself.
  Also: can end the whole program.

Function first_code
  Takes: a (list of whole numbers, only read), n (whole number)
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    (exact step) Repeats up to n times, with i counting from 0:
         1. Checks the value of item i of a:
            If it is 7:
              1. Stops and gives back i.
            In any other case:
              (does nothing)
    Then gives back negative 1.
  Changes: nothing outside itself.

Function count_rows
  Takes: rows (whole number), cols (whole number)
  Gives back: a whole number
  What it does:
    (exact step) Creates seen (whole number), starting at 0.
    (exact step) Repeats rows times, with r counting from 0:
         1. Repeats up to cols times, with c counting from 0:
              1. If c is equal to 2:
                   1. Stops the loop.
              2. Adds 1 to seen.
    Then gives back seen.
  Changes: nothing outside itself.

Function pick
  Takes: out (pointer to whole number), big (whole number)
  What it does:
    - the location out points to becomes 100 if big is not zero, otherwise 1
  Changes: only what out points to.
